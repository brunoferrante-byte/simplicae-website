06 · Forecast

The three year runway

One season is a snapshot; ownership invests in a trajectory. Set your revenue and cost growth and the model projects the path — showing exactly when the club reaches break even and how much funding the journey needs.

01 · Drivers

Set the drivers once — every line calculates from them

These shared quantities feed the calculations throughout Revenue and Expenses. Change home matches or average salary and every linked line recalculates live. Each ledger line shows its own formula, which you can edit inline or override with a fixed value.

02 · Revenue

Income — every line is a calculation

Yellow-edged factors are linked to a driver (edit them on the Drivers tab). White factors are the line's own rates. Toggle Fixed to override any line with a flat number.

03 · Expenses

The full operation — calculated

All 12 categories. Player salaries calculate as roster × average and count against the cap; travel calculates from away matches × party × airfare; and so on. Contingency scales as a % of everything above it.

04 · Dashboard

The whole club on one screen

Live from every driver and line. The owner subsidy is the plug that covers the gap between earned revenue and total cost.
